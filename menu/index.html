What's baking

Everything here is made to order in small batches. Pick what you'd like, then send an order by email or Instagram and Megan will confirm a date.

Cinnamon Rolls

Soft, pillowy dough rolled up with brown sugar and cinnamon, baked until the edges turn golden, then finished with a vanilla-cream glaze. Best eaten warm. They rarely survive the drive home.

Made with:
flour, milk, brown sugar, heavy cream, butter, eggs, water, sugar, yeast, salt, vanilla, cinnamon
Contains:
wheat, milk, eggs

Good to know

Allergens. Cinnamon rolls contain wheat, milk, and eggs. Everything is made in a home kitchen that also handles nuts and other allergens, so it is not a safe choice for severe allergies.

Made to order. Nothing sits on a shelf. Please give at least two days' notice so Megan can plan her bakes.

Pickup. Orders are handed over at a public meet-up spot around Draper, Utah. There is no shipping.

Utah Food Freedom Act. Made in a home kitchen that is not licensed or inspected by the Utah Department of Agriculture and Food or a local health department.
