How ordering works

No cart, no checkout, no account. You talk to the person who bakes your order.

  1. Tell us what you'd like

    Email orders@hearthhoneybakery.com or send a DM on Instagram with what you want, how many, and the day you'd like them. The order page writes the email for you.

  2. Megan bakes it fresh

    She confirms your date and payment details, then bakes your order fresh for that day. Please give at least two days' notice.

  3. Meet-up handoff

    We meet at a public spot around Draper and hand over the goods, still warm if the timing works out. Bring napkins.
